Please review the following policies before confirming your order with GRAFIX.

01 / QUOTATIONS

Quotes depend on your requirements.

Product pricing may vary depending on product type, size, material, finishing, quantity and customisation.

Customers can contact GRAFIX through WhatsApp or other available contact channels to receive a quotation.

A quotation is valid only for the specifications discussed with the customer.

02 / PAYMENT

Orders are fully prepaid.

Full payment must be completed before production begins.

Production will not begin until the payment has been confirmed by GRAFIX.

Payment confirmation does not mean that the order can be cancelled automatically.

03 / CUSTOM PRODUCTS

Please check your details carefully.

Custom products may include names, photographs, artwork, logos, dates, contact details and other customer- provided information.

Customers are responsible for checking spelling, names, dates, phone numbers, artwork and other supplied details before production.

GRAFIX is not responsible for mistakes caused by incorrect information approved or supplied by the customer.

04 / PRODUCTION

Production begins after confirmation.

Once the quotation, specifications and payment have been confirmed, the order may proceed to production.

Production time depends on the product, quantity, customisation and production requirements.

Estimated completion or dispatch time will be communicated where applicable.

05 / CANCELLATION

Confirm before production.

Orders may not be cancelled once production has started.

For customised products, cancellation may not be possible after production or customisation work has begun.

Customers should confirm all product specifications before making payment.

06 / REFUNDS

Refunds are subject to the order status.

Payments for customised or production- based orders are generally non-refundable once production has started.

If a production issue is caused by GRAFIX, the issue will be reviewed and an appropriate resolution may be offered depending on the circumstances.

Refund eligibility, where applicable, will be decided based on the specific order and its production status.

07 / DELIVERY

Delivery is through courier.

GRAFIX does not provide home delivery through its own delivery service.

Orders requiring delivery may be sent through a courier service.

Courier or delivery charges are separate from the product quotation unless specifically stated otherwise.

Courier delivery times may vary depending on the destination and courier service.

08 / INSTALLATION

Installation is not provided.

GRAFIX does not provide installation services for products supplied by us.

Customers are responsible for arranging installation or mounting where required.

Installation-related labour, tools, mounting materials or other related expenses are not included unless specifically agreed in writing.

09 / PRODUCT VARIATION

Printed products may vary slightly.

Actual colours may vary slightly from colours displayed on screens due to differences in monitors, mobile displays, printing processes and materials.

Minor differences in colour, texture, finishing or material appearance may occur between the digital preview and the final physical product.

10 / CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY

Review everything before confirming.

Customers must ensure that the submitted content, artwork, photographs, logos, names, information and instructions are accurate and that they have the necessary rights or permissions to use them.

GRAFIX is not responsible for copyright, trademark or ownership disputes arising from content supplied by the customer.
